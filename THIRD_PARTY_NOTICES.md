# Third-party materials

The current trainer has no third-party JavaScript dependencies. Playback uses our own `audio.js` and browser APIs. Keep the following piano credit, license links and upstream notice with copies of the app.

## Piano recordings

**Salamander Grand Piano**, **Alexander Holm**, [CC BY 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) ([legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode)). Source: [Tone.js audio repository](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander); original notice retained in `licenses/Salamander-README.txt`. That notice also credits Markus Fiedler for retuning.

The app streams 17 unchanged MP3s from https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/: C2, Ds2, Fs2, A2, C3, Ds3, Fs3, A3, C4, Ds4, Fs4, A4, C5, Ds5, Fs5, A5, C6. It selects this subset and adjusts pitch, duration and gain envelopes at playback. The recordings are of a Yamaha C5. No endorsement by the authors, Yamaha or Casio is claimed. The CC license applies to the recordings and their adaptations; it is not a license for unrelated trainer code.

## Interface and prose

No external font files, image assets or icon libraries are shipped. Interval names and semitone counts are musical facts. The descriptive hints in this release were rewritten during the September 2026 audit. Original baseline material and development history are excluded by the publication script because their provenance has not been independently established.
